01
SYSTEMATIC REVIEW + PILOT EXPERIMENTS

First, understand where the system breaks.

A PRISMA-guided review of 61 studies organizes RAG failures into four groups: missing context, context noise, formatting errors, and hallucination despite retrieval. Pilot Arabic and Malay experiments help frame the next questions.

Read the review ↗
02
CONTROLLED EXPERIMENTS · MANUSCRIPT

A stronger score is not always a better answer.

Five-seed experiments on Qwen3 and T5Gemma 2 isolate an NLI reward from fine-tuning and its surrounding machinery. After length matching, the reward provides no reliable additional benefit in the tested setting.

Read the controlled study ↗
03
PLANNED WORK · PHD PROPOSAL

Test mitigation under imperfect evidence.

The next study plans to compare length-controlled preference optimization and evidence-grounded training under distractors, missing evidence, and retrieval variation. Improved performance is a question to test, not an assumed result.

Explore the research proposal ↗

04 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

A researcher.
An educator.

I’m Akram Taha Zeyad, a PhD candidate at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia, researching proxy validity and controlled mitigation in evidence-grounded multilingual generation for Arabic and Malay, supervised by Dr. Lailatul Qadri Zakaria and Dr. Saidah Saad.

Alongside my research, I’m an Assistant Lecturer at the University of Technology–Iraq. My work brings together language technology, practical machine learning, and teaching.
